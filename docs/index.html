Booking Status Update: how it works

Live since 21 November 2026. Providers get WhatsApp messages with buttons. One tap tells us where a visit stands, updates the booking in Zoho, shows on the Live Status tab, and keeps the customer informed automatically. No typing, no chasing.

Where a booking comes from

Before any message goes out, a booking has to reach Zoho and be marked Scheduled.

1

Where it starts

A customer orders in the Pawkit app (the booking carries a Booking Code), or the team creates the booking in Zoho from a WhatsApp chat or enquiry.

2

Booking record in Zoho CRM

Every booking lives in the Booking module. Before it is fixed it sits in New enquiry, Pending Provider Time Slot or Pending Owner Confirmation. Nothing is sent yet.

3

Status becomes Scheduled

This is the switch. The booking needs a visit date and time, a provider with a WhatsApp number, and the customer's mobile number.

4

Booking Status Update starts

Zoho sets timers from the visit time. No one switches it on per booking. The journey below runs from here.

  • Booked late? Steps whose time has already passed are skipped. A same-day booking gets no day-before reminder.
  • Missing a number? No provider number means no provider messages. No customer mobile means no customer messages. Fix the number on the booking.
  • When it stops. Once the booking is Completed, Cancelled or otherwise no longer Scheduled, no more messages go out.
  • Which bookings are covered. Home visits get every message on this page: Grooming, Vaccination, Home Consultation, General health check, Microchip, Euthanasia, Medical Sitting, Dog walking, Pet sitting and Pet Transport.
  • No home visit, so left out: Teleconsult, Remote Service and AQC Consultation (the vet goes to the Animal Quarantine Centre). These bookings get no status messages, no late checks, and do not show in Live Status. When CS closes one as Completed, the customer still gets the survey (completion check and rating).

CS team

  • Watch Live Status. Each visit shows its step, and "Needs the CS team" lists what to act on.
  • Work the Provider Status tasks in the Task Tracker like any other task.
  • Late arrivals follow the Late Compensation SOP in the CS Handbook. The $20 credit is sent automatically; you get a task when it was not, and when a customer with an unused credit books again.
  • When you message a provider yourself on WhatsApp and they reply, set Visit Status on the booking with the Update button.
  • When a provider is late, the customer is told automatically once the provider confirms the delay by tapping a reason. Live Status then shows "Customer told at" with the time. If it still says "Waiting for the provider to confirm" after a few minutes, message the customer the new arrival time yourself.
  • Late-arrival credit, automatic. If the provider confirms a delay of 30 to 60 minutes, whatever the reason, the customer also gets a WhatsApp saying a $20 service credit has been added for the next booking, valid for 3 months, one per late booking (Escalation SOP, section 4). The credits are listed under Service Satisfaction. Over 60 minutes late or a no-show stays with CS: offer a free priority rebooking.
  • Compensation tasks for CS, automatic. You get a task when: a provider taps Arrived 30 minutes or more after the booked time and no credit was sent; a provider said they were coming but has not tapped Arrived 60 minutes after the booked time (possible no-show); or a customer with an unused $20 late credit makes a new booking. Each task says what to offer. The steps are in the Late Compensation SOP in the CS Handbook.
  • Still close each booking in Zoho (Completed, Cancelled or To be Rescheduled). A tap does not close it.

Providers

  • Messages come from the Pawkit WhatsApp line, the same one as today.
  • Tap one button per message. Nothing to type.
  • If anything changes, reply in the same chat and CS will pick it up.

Good to know in week one

  • Providers get two sets of reminders for now. The older reminders without buttons are still on until we switch them off.
  • Customers are kept informed automatically. "On the way" with a live tracking link, "about 10 minutes away", a delay message after the provider confirms one, a $20 service credit when that delay is 30 to 60 minutes, and after the visit a completion check with a 1 to 5 rating and a comment. Customers are not told separately when the provider arrives.
  • Running-late rules notice: once per provider, no blast. A provider gets the rules notice (with an "I have read this" button) together with their first visit message from now on. Anyone who already has it (replied or tapped on 23 Nov) never gets it again. Switch: Zoho variable Lateness_Notice_Per_Booking (on / test / off); sent date is on the Partner record (Lateness Notice Sent At). Ops360 › Provider Status shows who has it and who tapped "I have read this".
  • Everything runs on cs360.pawkit.sg. Providers' trip page: cs360.pawkit.sg/track/go.html. Customers' tracking page: cs360.pawkit.sg/track. Older links that still say pawslyops.github.io forward there automatically.
  • Late arrivals are recorded on every visit (from 24 Nov). Over 10 minutes late counts: 1 point, over 25 = 2, over 40 = 3 (Provider Accountability & Penalty Framework, section D, approved by Charlene on 23 Nov). Lateness is measured from the new time only when the provider warned more than 60 minutes ahead, the delay is up to 60 minutes and the customer tapped "Yes, that's fine". Wrong address, building access and "customer asked to delay" are not marked. A provider who never taps Completed gets a reminder first; later misses count. Points are recorded, and Operations applies any deduction after review. For now marks are recorded quietly: providers are not messaged about each mark yet.
  • "10 minutes away" is sent on a timer, not from the provider's location. It goes out 10 minutes before the expected arrival once the provider has tapped On my way.
  • A tap only counts from the provider on the booking. If the number on the booking is wrong, the status will not update. Fix the number in Zoho.
